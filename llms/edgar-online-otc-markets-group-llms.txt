# Edgar Online (OTC Markets Group)

> Edgar Online (OTC Markets Group) is a fintech company profile in the API Evangelist network, surfaced from the bain-capital-ventures VC portfolio graph. Edgar Online historically provided online access to SEC EDGAR filings and derived financial data; OTC Markets Group operates financial markets and market-data services for over-the-counter securities. This profile is an enrichment lead — no first-party OpenAPI, SDK, or developer surface has been captured for it yet.
